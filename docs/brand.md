# AuRoom approved brand system

The customer-approved visual reference is the black-and-gold AuRoom lockup committed under `frontend/public/brand/`.

## Brand assets

- `frontend/public/brand/auroom-lockup.webp` — compact web derivative of the approved lockup for splash/loading presentation.
- `frontend/public/brand/auroom-symbol.webp` — exact cropped symbol from the same approved artwork for small UI placements.

Do not recreate the cube mark with CSS shapes or substitute another icon when the approved symbol can be used.

## Palette sampled from the approved artwork

- Brand black: `#030807`
- Soft black: `#0A1012`
- Gold: `#E4C67E`
- Strong gold highlight: `#F1D385`
- Deep gold: `#C99F51`
- Warm ivory text: `#F5F0E3`
- Muted warm neutral: `#A89F8D`

Gold is the only primary accent. Blue-green, terracotta, orange and unrelated accent systems must not be introduced into primary AuRoom surfaces without explicit approval.

## UI direction

The application should feel like the digital presentation board of a premium architectural studio: near-black presentation surfaces, restrained warm gold technical lines, high contrast content, and minimal decoration. The questionnaire remains a focused architectural working sheet rather than a generic rounded-card SaaS wizard.

Accessibility and interaction states may use lighter/darker values derived from the approved palette as long as they preserve readable contrast and do not create a new competing brand color.
